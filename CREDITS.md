# The Lion King: Ultimate Edition

**Ultimate Edition by [Linn1024](https://github.com/Linn1024).**

Ultimate Edition is developed and maintained as a separate project, including
its SNES/Genesis resource mixing, customization, expanded views and Windows
distribution.

Project and source: [LionKing-Ultimate-Edition](https://github.com/Linn1024/LionKing-Ultimate-Edition).

## Based on

**[TLKRecomp](https://github.com/kinduff/TLKRecomp) by Alejandro AR (kinduff)
and contributors** is the upstream PC-port foundation.
[Upstream author's website](https://kinduff.com).

## Components and original game

- snesrecomp: recompiler/runtime foundation.
- recomp-ui and Dear ImGui: menu framework and UI components.
- SDL3: windowing, input and audio.
- Genesis Plus GX: included backend, with its matching source and license.
- Original game: Disney / Virgin Interactive and its original developers.
