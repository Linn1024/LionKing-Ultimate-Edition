# The Lion King: Ultimate Edition

Your favourite parts of SNES and Genesis, together on PC.

## [Download for Windows](https://github.com/Linn1024/LionKing-Ultimate-Edition/releases/download/windows-2026-11-21/LionKing-Ultimate-Edition-Windows.zip)

- Mix artwork, colours, music and gameplay from both versions.
- Change character and scenery artwork while you play.
- Play in widescreen and save your own presets.

## Start playing

1. Download and extract the ZIP.
2. Open **Setup.cmd** and select your **SNES USA** and **Genesis / Mega Drive World** ROMs.
3. Open **Play.cmd**.

Press **F12** to customize your game. ROMs are not included.
