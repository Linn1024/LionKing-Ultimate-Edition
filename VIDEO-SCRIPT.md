# Video script — Lion King: Your Edition

Target: about 2–3 minutes. Show actual gameplay and the actual F12 menu. Record
short clips rather than a complete playthrough. Default to The best preset,
a moderate wide window and a clean recording without the FPS overlay.

| Time | Picture / action | Voice or caption |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:12 | A short Pride Lands gameplay clip. Title overlay: “The Lion King — Your Edition”. | “What if you could choose your favourite parts of the SNES and Genesis versions of The Lion King?” |
| 0:12–0:28 | Show the three launch choices, then a short clip with The best preset. | “This Windows fan port lets you mix their artwork, colours, music, sound effects and gameplay.” |
| 0:28–0:55 | Open F12. Select a hyena branch. Show the complete SNES/Genesis previews, then the separate artwork and palette choices. Apply a visible change and resume. | “Choose a whole style, or customize one character. Artwork and colours are independent. The previews show what you are choosing.” |
| 0:55–1:15 | In Music & sound, play one SNES preview, pause, then its Genesis counterpart. Leave narration silent while each sample plays. | “You can choose the soundtrack and individual sound effects too.” |
| 1:15–1:35 | Show one gameplay comparison with a clear existing description. Show the restart notice, then return to play. | “Gameplay has separate choices. The menu explains the difference and tells you when a level needs to restart.” |
| 1:35–1:55 | Three brief clips from King, Graveyard and an adult level. Show a moderate expanded view. | “Play through the game with your own mix. Resizable expanded views are supported, though very large windows still need work.” |
| 1:55–2:15 | Open Presets, save a named preset and show it in the list. | “Save your mix as a preset, and share the preset file with friends.” |
| 2:15–2:35 | Show Setup's two Browse fields. Do not show personal file paths. Cut to Play.cmd. | “Download the Windows preview, extract it, and run Setup. Supply your own SNES USA and Genesis World ROMs. Then press Play. No compiler is needed.” |
| 2:35–2:45 | Gameplay outro; final card with the actual repository/releases URL once published. | “This is an unfinished, noncommercial fan project. Try it, report bugs, and help us make it better.” |

Record the F12 shots with `Record Video.cmd --desktop`, since customization is a
separate window. For game-only shots use the default. Record both sound previews
without narration; add voice-over during editing. Do not imply every resource
has an equivalent in both originals or that extreme resolutions are fully fixed.

Before uploading: check the final video has sound, legible text, no personal
paths/desktop notifications, and an accurate download link. Keep it free to watch
and retain the original game and component credits in the description.
